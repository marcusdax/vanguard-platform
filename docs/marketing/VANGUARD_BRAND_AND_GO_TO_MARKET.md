## 1. Executive Brand Positioning

**Vanguard** is a dual-surface physical verification, hyper-local service dispatch, and decentralized spatial telemetry network. Paired with **Vanguard Field** (Android client `com.vanguard.field`), the ecosystem bridges everyday neighborhood assistance with institutional-grade cryptographic evidence.

Core brand promise across every audience:

- **For Neighborhood Residents (`/community` — Vanguard Neighborhood):** Plain-English, flat-rate local assistance from identity-verified neighbors, protected by a 4-digit verbal arrival PIN and optional photo-royalty sharing.
- **For Field Operators (`/` & `Vanguard Field` — Vanguard Tactical):** Zero-paywall day-one earning (`0 bps` operator fee deducted from earned bounties), H3 1.5-mile micro-zone batching engineered around IRS `$0.76/mi` vehicle costs, and a free 24-module Open Academy that turns floor work into certified technical credentials and funded hardware.
- **For Enterprise Buyers (Carriers, Restoration Contractors, Solar & Supply Chain):** Court-ready, tamper-evident field dossiers (`SHA-256` canonical record chains, `RFC 6962` Merkle inclusion proofs, and nonce-bound hardware attestation) backed by strict statutory neutrality boundaries.
